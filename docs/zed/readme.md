# devsimsek's dotfiles - zed

Zed is my favorite full fledged editor choice. It's fast, minimal, and has a lot of the features I want without being bloated. Also, I have met with some of its builders and they are very nice people. I highly recommend checking it out if you are looking for a new editor.

## Quick overview of what have I changed

Well to summarize, I have changed mostly the keymap; other parts except AI usage and some dock positioning are mostly untouched. I have also added some custom snippets for my own usage. Like always, I am using Rose Pine theme, and I have also added some custom colors to it.
